# Engineering Vision

## Purpose

Define how software engineering is practiced within Anverra Global.

---

# Vision

Engineering shall prioritize correctness, maintainability, and long-term evolution over short-term implementation speed.

---

# Objectives

Engineering shall produce software that is:

- Modular
- Testable
- Observable
- Secure
- Well documented
- Continuously improvable

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Architecture guides implementation.

Business requirements drive architecture.

Technology supports business objectives.

Automation supports engineering.

Artificial Intelligence accelerates engineering but does not replace engineering judgment.
