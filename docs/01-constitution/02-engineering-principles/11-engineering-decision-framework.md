# 1. Purpose

This document defines the framework for making engineering decisions within AEOS.

The purpose is to ensure that important engineering decisions are:

- intentional
- evidence-based
- aligned with business and product goals
- consistent with AEOS principles
- explicit
- traceable
- appropriately governed
- validated where necessary

The framework applies to decisions involving:

- architecture
- technology
- product implementation
- engineering processes
- AI-assisted engineering
- security
- operations
- standards
- workflows
- significant implementation trade-offs

---

# 2. Core Principle

> **Important engineering decisions should be explicit, reasoned, traceable, and aligned with intent.**

AEOS identifies **Explicit Decisions** as one of its engineering laws. :contentReference[oaicite:18]{index=18}
