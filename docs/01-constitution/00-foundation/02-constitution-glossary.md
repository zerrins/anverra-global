# Purpose

Define the canonical terminology used throughout the Engineering Constitution.

---

# Terms

## Constitution

The highest governing engineering authority.

---

## Principle

A long-term engineering belief that guides decisions.

---

## Standard

A mandatory engineering rule derived from one or more principles.

---

## Specification

A detailed description of what shall be implemented.

---

## Architecture

The structural organization of the software system.

---

## Business Capability

A business responsibility owned by a single domain.

---

## Module

A deployable or logical implementation of a business capability.

---

## AI Skill

A reusable engineering capability executed by Artificial Intelligence.

---

## Workflow

An orchestration of multiple AI skills.

---

## ADR

Architecture Decision Record documenting significant engineering decisions.

---

# Rules

Every constitutional document shall use glossary terminology consistently.
